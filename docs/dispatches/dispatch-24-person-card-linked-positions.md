## Source audit and bounded repairs

- The old editor wrote descriptive Job Title into both `jobTitle` and legacy Person `role`, sent Department and compatibility District text on every save, and mixed work/support links with independently owned management assignments. The new editor writes changed fields only; title edits never rewrite legacy `role`. Historical aliases/compatibility territory text are visible and preserved, not presented as canonical assignments. Department remains explicitly descriptive; no invented department registry.
- Internal `positionIds` links a Person to stable catalog IDs: `store-manager`, `assistant-store-manager`, `key-holder`, `district-manager`, `regional-manager`, `other`. Any intentional concurrent subset is supported. Position changes require a dependency preview and acknowledgement; no automatic ownership, access, title or account changes. Removing a position required by a current responsibility is blocked until it is retained explicitly or that assignment is intentionally reassigned/vacated through the existing management workflow.
- Omitted `positionIds` means unmigrated legacy title discovery. Suggestions are labeled and are not persisted by unrelated edits. Explicit empty arrays mean no mapped positions; invalid controlled mappings never fall back to title. Unknown/duplicate position IDs are rejected when changed. Older writers omitting the field preserve its saved value. No automatic backfill/cutover is performed. Legacy explicit cross-role discovery remains available for unmapped records; mapped People obey their controlled position requirements even in show-all mode. This is an opt-in compatibility phase, not complete uniform migration of all historic data.
- Shared checks cover mapped-person new assignments through direct writes, approval results, CSV commit/preview and District handover. Retained historical conflicts do not become new assignments. Mapped new leadership requires clearly Active lifecycle; inactive/contradictory/unknown states fail closed. Final-state validation covers simultaneous position/assignment writes in either order. Server remains authoritative inside the existing transaction.
- The card has Profile & positions, Contact & privacy, Work locations, Management responsibilities, and Lifecycle & account access sections. Every input is labeled; selected support chips survive search filtering. The fixed modal footer does not overlap scrolling mobile content. Close/Cancel/Escape guard edited drafts and refuse dismissal during saving. Validation errors receive focus rather than appearing silently below the scroll area. The Organization link opens the actual hierarchy tab.
- Canonical District ownership is shown separately, including zero-store Districts, retired references and unresolved parents. District-owned DMs override copied retail-store labels for dependency display/position checks; stale copies are diagnostics, not management authority. Store-owned SM/AM/KH/RM and legacy DM responsibilities remain explicit. New position selection never assigns a District or silently removes an old store. The existing District ownership writer remains administrator-only.
- Inactivation previews blockers and stays separate from account revocation. Zero-store District leadership is a blocker too. Linked Directory accounts are visible but not edited; no external access is changed. Full atomic offboarding/acting coverage and automatic replacement remain future work, not claimed complete.
- Work contacts are separate from login and Location inboxes. Matching a shared inbox generates a warning without rewriting identity. Missing/historic phone visibility is not silently mapped to a new value; changed visibility is server-validated and older omitted values are preserved. Existing email alias/clear semantics remain intact.
- Duplicate Person submits are locked. Empty/partial/duplicate/mismatched or wrong-version save responses cannot claim success, including multi-record leadership-copy responses. Creation uses a UUID ID and create-only expected version, giving the response a verifiable version. Missing loaded People no longer return a false success. Definite rejection preserves drafts; uncertain saves lock repeat submission and offer a separate read-only saved-record tab. No new Person mutation receipt/cancellation protocol is claimed.
